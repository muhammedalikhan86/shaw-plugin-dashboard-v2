What is switched off on the test site

  1. Electronic signing (Annature). Notices can be issued, but they are not sent out for signing.
  2. Filing in DART. Signed documents are not filed, and there are no DART links.
  3. Emails go to one test mailbox. Nothing reaches a client, Adviser or team: every email goes to the UAT testers' mailbox, with [UAT] at the start of the subject.
  4. The Adviser Dashboard shows the OFA screens only. Its money figures are left empty.
  5. All data is made up. There are no real S-Accounts and no real financial figures.
